Graduate Computational Urban Designer (G2) - Bristol / Cardiff
Arup Careers
https://jobs.arup.com/jobs/graduate-computational-urban-designer-g2-bristol-34810

Start: 1 September 2027
Advert closing: 12 November 2026 (may close early)

The Opportunity

The Graduate Computational Urban Designer would contribute across two connected workstreams:

Strategic Masterplanning & Advisory:
- Masterplanning and urban design studies
- Research, analysis and visualisation
- Development frameworks and strategic plans
- Community and stakeholder engagement support
- Bid and proposal support

Urban InForm:
- Project deployment and set-up
- Computational modelling support
- Testing and quality assurance with Arup domain experts
- Development of re-usable digital workflows and libraries
- User support, onboarding and training materials
- Production of client demonstrations and project value stories

https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/inform/

Is this role right for you?

A Master's-level degree (or equivalent) in a relevant discipline, such as Computer Science (including Cybersecurity), Architecture, Urban Design or Urban Planning.

- Parametric and computational design: Rhino and Grasshopper. Ability to build, test and maintain reusable scripts, definitions and workflows. Understanding of spatial parameters, design rules, optimisation and scenario testing.
- GIS, spatial data and mapping: ArcGIS, QGIS and/or FME. Spatial data preparation, cleaning, geoprocessing and analysis. Ability to work with site, land use, movement, environmental and socio-economic datasets.
- Data analytics and visualisation: Excel, Power BI and/or similar. Interpret datasets and translate outputs into clear project insights. Basic understanding of data structures, KPIs and performance metrics. Urban modelling and scenario testing: masterplan option testing, density studies, accessibility analysis, land-use mix, capacity studies and climate/sustainability metrics. Connect design inputs with evidence-based outputs for client decision-making.
- Coding and scripting: Python particularly useful for automation, data processing and linking design tools with external datasets. Familiarity with APIs, notebooks or low-code/no-code workflows would be beneficial.
- CAD and design production: AutoCAD, Rhino and potentially Revit awareness. Translate between technical outputs and spatial/design drawings.
- Graphic communication: Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop). Clear visual storytelling for reports, bids, client demonstrations and training materials.
- AI-supported workflows: Interest in AI-assisted design, research, coding, data interpretation and visualisation. Ability to test AI-enabled workflows responsibly and document their value, risks and limitations.

Core attributes: Changemakers; Innovative minds; Growth seekers; Adaptable adventurers.

Application must include academic transcript. Upload evidence of work (portfolio/website), PDF, max 20MB.

Eligibility: full working rights; no more than 12 months full-time comparable experience; recently graduated or on track to graduate from most recent degree before employment starts. No visa sponsorship.
